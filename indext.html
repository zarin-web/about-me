About me

My Name is Zarin Shirzad

I was born in 2001 in Faryab, Afghanistan. I graduated from Setara High School and took the university entrance exam.

I passed the exam and was accepted into the Economics department. Unfortunately, universities were closed, so I had to register at an institute because I did not want to stay at home without studying.

However, when I was in my fourth semester, the institutes were also closed. I was very sad, but I never lost hope. I continued learning at home and studied English.

Through social media, I learned about organizations that provide educational opportunities for Afghan girls. I applied to several organizations, and after a long wait, I finally received a response from Afghan Geeks.

I am very happy to have this opportunity to study my favorite subject. I am grateful to Afghan Geeks for helping me continue my education and work toward my dreams.

Thank you, Afghan Geeks!
